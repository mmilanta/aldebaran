Study 001 · Three circles make a garden

2-fold symmetry. The curve repeats twice around its center.

The point sits at the end of three turning arms. Each arm rotates at a different speed; their movements add together to make the line. Show the arms to follow the mechanism; saved drawings contain only the curve.

Where do the folds come from?

The first arm turns once. The outer arm turns forwards; the inner arm turns backwards. Count their turns relative to the first arm: subtract 1 from the outer rhythm and add 1 to the inner rhythm.

For 7 and 13, that gives 6 and 14. Their greatest common divisor is 2, so there are 2 equal sectors.

After each sector, the arms have the same arrangement, rotated together around the center. Color follows the point through the full cycle.
